Selected work

Bill Swipe

Mobile app, in progress

A mobile app that lets someone who can’t cover a bill share it, and lets a stranger pay it. The money goes straight to the biller (electricity, DStv, MTN), never to the person. Bills are verified with the biller, meter numbers are encrypted, and each payment is confirmed through Flutterwave before the biller is paid exactly once.

About

I’m a front-end developer based in Nigeria, and I’m now growing into full-stack development. I’ve built and deployed a real estate website, and I’m currently working on Bill Swipe, a mobile app that handles payments, verifies bills with the biller and protects users’ private details.

I’m also training as a cybersecurity analyst and taking an ethical hacking course. That shapes how I build: I check inputs, encrypt sensitive data and confirm every payment before money moves.

Beyond code, I teach. I run digital literacy sessions for students, covering safe internet use, the basics of AI and how to start with entrepreneurship.

I build with AI-assisted tools to move fast, and I’m learning the fundamentals underneath so I understand what I ship. I’m looking for opportunities where I can keep growing on real products.
